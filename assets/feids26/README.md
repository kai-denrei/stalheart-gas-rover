# FEIDS-26 / Fusiform Expendable Interplanetary Delivery System

Expendable interplanetary cargo shell, designed as a projectile launched between planets. A 20.16 m long, 7.5 m maximum-diameter fusiform spindle has matching pointed ends and a continuous convex taper from its widest middle and four rotationally identical shell quarters. Grey covers the upper (local -Z) half and black the lower leading (local +Z) half in nose-down flight. The yellow tip is removed; its old telemetry name remains as an empty compatibility node. The four curved quarters separate radially to release cargo. This prototype presents terminal deployment; it does not simulate launch energy, interplanetary navigation, atmospheric aerodynamics or thermal protection performance.

Two intact MX-06 Ridgebacks at 0.75 scale fit in tandem, with folded hydraulic legs. The cinematic uses two capsules for four mechs. Cargo remains separate and references the original Ridgeback files; no source mech export is modified. Healthy detailed/game cargo fits inside the inner shell with at least 0.808 m sampled vertex clearance and 0.313 m conservative longitudinal separation. Dented/breached shell states are visual damage alternatives, not certified transport clearances.

## Runtime contract

Plain GLB is the source of truth. Coordinates: metres, +Y up, +Z nose/forward. Root is on the ground below the shell centre; geometric axis is Y = 3.8 m. Socket roots: `SOCKET_CARGO_1` at (0, 2.05, -2.2), `SOCKET_CARGO_2` at (0, 2.05, 2.2). Apply 0.75 cargo scale to wrappers, preserving original rig nodes. `SOCKET_CENTER` identifies the shell axis.

`SHELL_QUADRANT_1` through `SHELL_QUADRANT_4` are independently driven engine pivots. `Shell_Separate` is a four-second one-shot applying a sharp radial separation impulse in the first 0.2 seconds, followed by outward/aft drift and tumbling. Play the clip OR call `applyShell(root,time,seed=26)`, never both. D0–D2 detailed/game tiers include it; D3 and LOD2 are static. All original node/socket names remain present across tiers and damage states; lookup-only nodes in LOD2 cannot animate its merged mesh. Keep original coordinates when instancing.

LOD0 is the recording master (D0 5,696 triangles, four draws, 234,172 bytes). LOD1 is the articulated game shell (1,568 triangles, four draws, 126,428 bytes). LOD2 is a static closed distance/loading shell (864 triangles, one draw, 30,324 bytes). Fetch distance first; swap near 150 m with 20 m hysteresis and always swap before shell separation. No measured phone FPS or speedup claim. Manifest contains all per-state measurements and source hashes; Meshopt copies are derived and validated after decoding.

Damage is independent from LOD: D0 intact; D1 scorched ceramic; D2 dented quadrant; D3 breached shell without deployment animation. The viewer defaults to D0 and prevents D3 playback. Broad collider suggestion: closed fusiform envelope from the profile in `runtime.js`; disable it before cargo release and use separate shell-quarter colliders. Collision response and debris cleanup belong to the game.

## Linked scene

Open `../../feids26/` for two-capsule deployment, packed cargo cutaway, or split-shell inspection. The packed cutaway hides quadrant 2 for inspection only. Scene `FEIDS_Deployment` lasts 28 seconds: entry first, violent splits at 6.2/6.65 s, approximately 300 m above ground, with brief controlled white flashes. The four releases occur at 6.2, 6.38, 6.65 and 6.83 s, then leg unfolding, short braking jets and touchdown at 13.00, 13.43, 14.00 and 19.23 s. Cargo sockets hold the folded rigs until release. The nose remains down during entry, with a brighter white-blue bow glow and 24 blue ribbon flames following the exterior of the local +Z dark shield and streaming toward -Z. `shield-heat.js` adds viewer-only emissive heating to outward-facing black shield surfaces, warming through orange into blue-white near the tip and cooling after breakup. Grey surfaces and inner faces retain their cold palette. Source GLB materials remain cold; game integrations may adopt this optional material shader. Shell debris slows sharply and shoots upward relative to the falling mechs, then disperses/tumbles. The four landing X/Z positions are (-6,-4), (-3,4), (4,-3), (7,5) metres; each triggers its own recoil, dust and camera shake. Mechs fall for 6.0, 6.25, 6.55 and 11.6 seconds before braking, reusing the Heavy Rain re-entry flame pool. The camera focuses on the first three mechs for their landings, holds the grounded trio from 14.0 to 16.5 seconds, then transitions to a fixed 0.20 m ground view by 18 seconds before the laggard arrives; its closest claw is 0.81 m from the lens at compression. Last-unit smoke opacity is reduced to keep the claw readable. Ground cracks, smoke, hot trails, camera shake and star/sky transition are presentation VFX.

`feids26/timeline.js` owns cargo folding/landing and scene trajectories; `runtime.js` owns only shell separation. Original mech weapon axes remain engine-driven. Debris impulse, direction and three-axis tumble are seeded. Replay chooses a new seed; `?play=1&seed=26` reproduces a specific breakup, and scrubbing keeps the seed fixed. The baked shell clip uses seed 26. This is a procedural scene, not a new baked cargo-motion GLB. Scene controls retain the compact top-right HUD and native fullscreen on Play/Replay. `?play=1` starts an unobstructed full-viewport autoplay presentation; native fullscreen requires user interaction.
