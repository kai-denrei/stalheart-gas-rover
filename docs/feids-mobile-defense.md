# FEIDS-26 mobile defense

Each race opens with the two FEIDS-26 capsules / four MX-06 Ridgebacks deploying beside its finish area. The 28-second full-screen sequence follows the capsules, shell opening, first three touchdowns and late fourth landing. Race physics and the three-light countdown wait until it ends. Click Skip intro or press Esc/Enter to start with all four already deployed; P or the Pause button pauses/resumes. Restart replays the opening and resets the deployment. The Stage Maker also uses the authored finish position. If an authored stage leaves no safe apron, deployment is omitted.

The landing site reserves ground clearance, the full overhead descent corridor, future sinkhole footprints and the track approach. The original 28-second procedural asset sequence is retained: splits at 6.2 / 6.65 seconds and touchdowns at 13.00 / 13.43 / 14.00 / 19.23 seconds, with folded cargo, separating shell quarters, heated shields, flame ribbons, ventral braking jets, hydraulic compression, dust and ground cracks. The camera returns to the starting rover before the countdown. The HUD is hidden during the opening.

After the opening, all four settled mechs patrol separate sectors even when no aliens are nearby. Their target selection spreads hunters across available aliens; close-range hunters flank instead of stopping. It turns its body toward targets, avoids structures, the race surface, holes, racers and other mechs, and remains within 115 metres of its landing position. Original alternating-tripod IK and telescopic hydraulics run from distance travelled. Each mech has two independent six-socket Quiver pods, a forward-only rotor gun and an articulated Lancer. Each pod has its own acquisition delay, reload variation and rearm delay after its missile finishes, preventing recurring synchronized volleys. Body orientation leads the selected alien for the rotor, and the weapons track the same hunting target. Quivers use the existing Stalheart unlit ejection/drop/ignition/apex/dive flight; rounds, heat, spin-up, locks, beams, damage and explosions share the city-defense simulation. Rotor rounds follow their physical muzzle direction and require body alignment. Mechs are friendly, persistent support units; this version does not add mech health or destruction.

Audio uses the three supplied MP3s, copied unchanged. Each capsule re-entry track ends at its split, with a small explosion sound at opening. Each ten-second mech recording starts exactly four seconds before its own touchdown. Rubble starts 60 ms after touchdown. These voices are scheduled separately from the capped combat sound pool, attenuate with listener distance, and seek to the correct recording offset after pause/resume. The supplied recording's broad impact transient spans roughly 3.7–4.2 seconds; the visual hit is aligned to 4.0 seconds.

Assets are pinned at `jelaludo/SentryTowers_A6` commit `1b9a85e34bd2b1c3738d3a66ce3c0b1ff24c7e26`, with original runtime/timeline modules under `upstream/a6/` and hashes in `assets.lock.json`. Models by jelaludo. No main-game files were modified. `src/feids-effects.js` adapts the source Heavy Rain trajectories into two bounded instanced sprite batches (180 flame / 168 dust particles). Articulated game meshes remain active during movement and combat; distant idle units use the static one-draw LOD.

Validation: `node --test test/feids.test.mjs`, `npm run test:feids` and `npm run profile:feids`. Browser acceptance checks both courses, the complete intro/countdown handoff, button and keyboard skip, pause/resume, all four impacts, independent walking, all weapon types firing at live targets, staggered salvos, audio scheduling and seek offsets, clean reset and console errors. A local 1440 × 913 / DPR 1 live battle sample with 454 aliens and four active mechs measured 60 FPS, 5.85 ms simulation, 4.04 ms render submission and 3.13 ms GPU. This is a local sample, not a phone performance claim.

All 208 unit tests, pinned asset/module checks, the Pages build, both complete race runs and FEIDS browser acceptance on both courses passed after the intro/hunting update.
